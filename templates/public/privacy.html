Pulsea Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

What Pulsea Does

Pulsea is a social publishing workspace used to manage clients, campaigns, social account connections, media uploads, and publishing activity.

Information We Collect

We collect account and workspace information such as operator login details, client names, campaign content, uploaded media, connected social account identifiers, publishing statuses, and audit records.

When you connect a third-party platform such as Pinterest, Facebook, or Instagram, Pulsea stores the access tokens and account identifiers needed to publish content you request.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide the Pulsea service, authenticate users, connect social accounts, upload and store media, publish posts, display publishing history, troubleshoot errors, and protect the workspace from unauthorized access.

Third-Party Platforms

Pulsea only sends content to connected social platforms when an authorized user schedules, publishes, or retries a post. Use of those platforms is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and Security

Pulsea uses restricted access controls for operator and client areas. Media and connection data are stored for the purpose of operating the publishing workspace. Access tokens are treated as confidential credentials.

Data Retention

We keep workspace data for as long as it is needed to provide the service, maintain publishing history, comply with operational requirements, or until deletion is requested and reasonably possible.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, or disconnection of social account data by contacting us. Some records may need to be retained for security, audit, or legal reasons.
